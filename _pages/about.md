## Who am I?

I'm a Computer Engineering student at the [University of Tehran](https://ece.ut.ac.ir/en/ece), and I currently work as an AI Software Engineer at [Divar](https://divar.ir/).

My work is mostly about taking real problems users encounter and finding AI solutions for them—often embedding-based—so their experience on the platform feels smoother. I particularly enjoy that the problems are concrete and that their impact on real people is visible.

I also enjoy teaching and have been a teaching assistant for several courses at the University of Tehran over the past few years. It remains one of my favorite parts of the week.

## Research Interests

My interests include:

* Trustworthy machine learning
* Fairness and robustness
* Large language models
* Computational neuroscience

## Other Interests

Outside of work and study, I enjoy music, movies, reading, traveling, table tennis, and running.
